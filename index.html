RapidA11y – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

RapidA11y is a local accessibility testing tool. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

Information We Collect

RapidA11y does not collect any user information. All scanning happens locally in your browser.

How RapidA11y Works

• Runs locally in the browser
• Does not send scans to any server
• No analytics, no cookies, no tracking

Data Sharing

No data is collected, shared, sold, or transferred.
